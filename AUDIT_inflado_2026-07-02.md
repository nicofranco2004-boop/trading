## Uncovered surfaces worth reviewing

**1. FCI prices are absent from the ENTIRE backend analysis/AI/snapshot stack (Class D, systemic gap).**
`analysis_prep.fetch_ba_aware_prices` (backend/analysis_prep.py:51-75) and `snapshots_job._fetch_prices` (only yfinance + `_resolve_ar_bond_price`) never consult `pricing.fci.get_prices_for`. FCI prices live only in the `fci_prices` table, served exclusively by `main.get_prices`. Consequence: every consumer that valuates through `currency_context` / `_position_value_usd` (AI chat snapshot `insights.py`, `dashboard*.py`, `insights_attribution.py`, `position.py`, `behavioral.py`, `goals_diagnostic.py`, `wrapped.py`, AND the historical snapshots that feed Evolution + monthly reports) values every FCI at COST (P&L 0). Worse: for an FCI in an AR broker, `fetch_ba_aware_prices:64` requests `FCI:...BA` (`_price_is_ars` returns true → appends `.BA`), a key that can never resolve. Not an inflation, but a real value/consistency divergence: the frontend Positions page shows live cuotaparte gains while the Dashboard/chat/reports/evolution show the same fund flat at cost. This is the single biggest D-class blind spot and it was not in the frontend-heavy D coverage.

**2. FCI currency (moneda ARS vs USD) is never checked at valuation time (Class D).** `fci_prices` stores a `moneda` column, but no valuator reads it. A USD-denominated FCI (`Cocos Dólares Plus`, `IEB Renta Fija Dólar`, `Cocos Ahorro Dólares` — all in `BROKER_FCI_ALLOWLIST`) whose price is in USD gets `÷ MEP` if it lands in an AR-broker branch (valuation.js:876-878; snapshots_job.py:186-190; behavioral `_position_value_usd`), or an ARS FCI in a USD broker gets read as USD (Positions.jsx:891). Today the `trustMktValue` band mostly masks this by falling to cost (~1400× trips the clamp), so it surfaces as silent cost-fallback rather than a visible ×1400, but it is a latent mispricing that is one clamp-tweak away from showing wrong. Worth an explicit moneda-aware branch.

**3. `insights.py` (the AI chat snapshot's `total_equity`/`geo_value`/`holdings_agg`) and `dashboard._compute_pnl_pct_for_position` use the NAME-based `_is_ars_broker`, not the structural `_price_is_ars`/`_is_ar_usd_subbroker` (Class C).** These were flagged high, but note the residual: a CEDEAR (`asset_type=='CEDEAR'`) in a USD broker whose name has no AR hint and no `·` separator (matched by the laxa `_is_usd_subbroker` but NOT by the strict `_is_ar_usd_subbroker` nor `_is_ars_broker`) routes to the US ticker (line 419-420) at ~3-15× and can slip under the 50× `_trust_mkt_value_usd` clamp. The clamp only catches the ~×100 bond case, not a 3× CEDEAR/ADR-ratio case. This is the one place the C-class clamp does NOT save you.

**4. A 6th class not in A–E: numerator/denominator scope mismatch in weight/allocation %.** `position.py:99-104` computes `weight_pct = current_value_usd (market) / total_value (COST basis, honor_override=False, empty prices)`. When the portfolio has gains, every weight is inflated by the aggregate gain factor and weights don't sum to 100%. This is the confirmed E finding, but it's a distinct *class* (market-over-cost ratio) worth grepping for elsewhere — any `value_market / total_cost` denominator. (The other builders, `dashboard_top_holdings`/`dashboard_composition`, correctly use market/market.)
